# 记忆 / 专注 / 反拖延 Resources

所有链接均经代理抓取核实（2026-10-02）。出版社原页面多数拒绝爬虫，统一用 PubMed 链接，DOI 已核对。

## Knowledge

### A. 学习科学：提取、间隔、理想难度

- [Paper: _Test-Enhanced Learning_ by Roediger & Karpicke (Psychological Science, 2006)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/)
  自测 vs 重读的经典实验。一周后自测组 56%、重读组 42%；读 4 遍的组最自信但忘得最多。Use for: 第 1 课核心证据。全文 PDF：https://colinallen.dnsalias.org/Readings/2006_Roediger_Karpicke_PsychSci.pdf
- [Paper: _Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques_ by Dunlosky et al. (PSPI, 2013)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26173288/)
  十种学习技巧效用评级：高 = 练习测试、分散练习；中 = 精细询问、自我解释、交错；低 = 总结、划线、关键词记忆术、文本意象、重读。Use for: 任何"该用哪种方法"的决定。
- [Paper: _Distributed practice in verbal recall tasks_ by Cepeda et al. (Psychological Bulletin, 2006)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/)
  间隔效应元分析（317 个实验）。Use for: 间隔复习的底层证据。
- [Paper: _Spacing effects in learning: A temporal ridgeline of optimal retention_ by Cepeda et al. (Psychological Science, 2008)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19076480/)
  最优复习间隔约为目标保持期的 5~40%（1 周保持期 → 间隔 20~40%；1 年 → 5~10%）。Use for: 回答"隔多久复习一次"。
- [Website: _Six Strategies for Effective Learning_ by The Learning Scientists](https://www.learningscientists.org/downloadable-materials)
  六大策略海报（间隔、提取、精细加工、交错、具体例子、双重编码），CC 授权，无中文版。Use for: 速查卡配图。
- [Lab: _Bjork Learning and Forgetting Lab_ (UCLA)](https://bjorklab.psych.ucla.edu/research/)
  理想难度、存储强度/提取强度理论的出处。Use for: 解释"学得费劲 ≠ 学得差"。
- [Paper: _Illusions of competence in monitoring one's knowledge during study_ by Koriat & Bjork (JEP:LMC, 2005)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15755238/)
  答案在眼前时会高估自己"会了"。Use for: 流畅性错觉。

### B. 书与课程

- [Book: _Make It Stick_ by Brown, Roediger & McDaniel (2014)](https://book.douban.com/subject/30353486/)
  课程主教材，面向大众的提取/间隔/交错综述。Chinese edition: 《认知天性》（中信，2018）。Use for: 每课的延伸阅读首选。
- [Book: _A Mind for Numbers_ by Barbara Oakley (2014)](https://book.douban.com/subject/26895988/)
  专注/发散模式、组块、拖延，偏理工科学习者。Chinese edition: 《学习之道》（机械工业出版社；注意与维茨金同名书区分）。Use for: 程序员视角的学习方法。
- [Book: _How We Learn_ by Benedict Carey (2014)](https://book.douban.com/subject/27081766/)
  记者写的学习科学通俗版。Chinese edition: 《如何学习》（浙江人民出版社）。Use for: 轻松入门。
- [Course: _Learning How to Learn_ by Oakley & Sejnowski (Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/learning-how-to-learn)
  418 万学员、4.8 分；有中文版。简体中文授课版：https://www.coursera.org/learn/ruhe-xuexi 。Use for: 课程的"第 0 课"，免费旁听。

### C. 间隔重复软件

- [Docs: _Anki Manual_](https://docs.ankiweb.net/)
  官方手册，含简体中文社区翻译。Use for: 安装、FSRS 算法、卡片模板。
- [Article: _Twenty rules of formulating knowledge_ by Piotr Wozniak (SuperMemo, 1999)](https://www.supermemo.com/en/blog/twenty-rules-of-formulating-knowledge)
  制卡 20 条规则（不理解不记、最小信息原则、填空……）。Use for: 教怎样写好卡片。镜像：https://supermemo.guru/wiki/20_rules_of_knowledge_formulation
- [Essay: _Spaced Repetition for Efficient Learning_ by Gwern Branwen](https://gwern.net/spaced-repetition)
  间隔重复的证据与实践长文。Use for: 想深挖的延伸阅读。

### D. 记忆术的证据

- [Paper: _Routes to remembering_ by Maguire et al. (Nature Neuroscience, 2003)](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn988)
  记忆冠军没有超常智力或脑结构差异，靠空间策略。Use for: 破除"记忆力天生"。
- [Paper: _Mnemonic Training Reshapes Brain Networks_ by Dresler et al. (Neuron, 2017)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28279356/)
  普通人每天 30 分钟、练 40 天记忆宫殿，72 词回忆从 26 → 62，4 个月后仍保留大半。Use for: 记忆宫殿模块。数字来源：https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/750131

### E. 不要做的事：脑训练 App

- [Paper: _Do "Brain-Training" Programs Work?_ by Simons et al. (PSPI, 2016)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27697851/)
  只能提高被训练任务本身，几乎没有远迁移证据。Use for: 劝退 Lumosity 类 App。
- [Paper: _Is working memory training effective?_ by Melby-Lervåg & Hulme (Developmental Psychology, 2013)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22612437/)
  元分析：工作记忆训练无泛化。Use for: 解释为什么不练 n-back。

### F. 硬件：睡眠与运动

- [Review: _About Sleep's Role in Memory_ by Rasch & Born (Physiological Reviews, 2013)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23589831/)
  慢波睡眠重放并转移记忆，REM 稳定。Use for: "熬夜学 = 白学"。
- [Guidance: _About Sleep_ (CDC)](https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/index.html)
  18~60 岁成人每天 7 小时以上。Use for: 可执行的睡眠目标。
- [Paper: _Exercise training increases size of hippocampus and improves memory_ by Erickson et al. (PNAS, 2011)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21282661/)
  RCT：有氧运动使海马体积 +2%，空间记忆改善。Use for: 运动模块核心引用（用户已每周跑 4 次，这是现成优势）。

### G. 拖延

- [Paper: _The nature of procrastination_ by Piers Steel (Psychological Bulletin, 2007)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17201571/)
  691 个相关系数的元分析；强预测因子 = 任务厌恶、延迟、自我效能、冲动性、尽责性。提出时间动机理论。Use for: 拖延总纲课。
- [Site: _The Procrastination Equation_ by Piers Steel (procrastinus.com)](https://procrastinus.com/)
  动机 = 期望 × 价值 ÷（冲动性 × 延迟）的通俗版。Chinese edition: 《战拖行动》（北京联合，2019）。Use for: 把公式讲给非学术读者。
- [Paper: _Procrastination and the Priority of Short-Term Mood Regulation_ by Sirois & Pychyl (Social and Personality Psychology Compass, 2013)](https://doi.org/10.1111/spc3.12011)
  拖延是短期情绪修复，代价由未来的自己承担。Use for: "拖延不是时间管理问题"。
- [Site: _Procrastination Research Group_ by Timothy Pychyl (Carleton University)](https://procrastination.ca/)
  论文/播客归档。Chinese edition（Pychyl 的书）: 《战胜拖延症》（文化发展出版社，2018）。Use for: 通俗解读入口。
- [Paper: _I forgive myself, now I can study_ by Wohl, Pychyl & Bennett (Personality and Individual Differences, 2010)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2010.01.029)
  对上次拖延的自我宽恕与下次拖延减少相关（样本 134 名本科生，该数字为二手转引）。Use for: "自责会加重拖延"。
- [Paper: _Implementation intentions: Strong effects of simple plans_ by Gollwitzer (American Psychologist, 1999)](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.54.7.493)
  "如果情境 x 出现，我就做 y" 的计划形式。Use for: if-then 计划课。
- [Paper: _Implementation intentions and goal achievement: A meta-analysis_ by Gollwitzer & Sheeran (Adv. Exp. Soc. Psych., 2006)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1)
  94 项检验，d = 0.65（中到大）。Use for: 给 if-then 计划一个效应量。
- [Paper: _How are habits formed_ by Lally et al. (European Journal of Social Psychology, 2010)](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674)
  96 人 12 周；达到自动化的时间范围 18~254 天，漏做一次不影响进程。常被转引的"中位数 66 天"不在摘要里，引用时注明。Use for: "习惯要多久"。
- [Book (popular): _Atomic Habits_ by James Clear (2018)](https://jamesclear.com/atomic-habits)
  通俗综合，不当原始证据引用。Chinese edition: 《掌控习惯》。Use for: 行动工具箱。

### H. 注意力

- [Paper: _No Task Left Behind? Examining the Nature of Fragmented Work_ by Mark, Gonzalez & Harris (ACM CHI, 2005)](https://ics.uci.edu/~gmark/CHI2005.pdf)
  24 名信息工作者实地观察：被打断后当天恢复平均 25 分钟。注意：流传的"23 分 15 秒"不在原论文里，课程统一引"约 25 分钟"。Use for: 一次打断的真实代价。
- [Paper: _The Cost of Interrupted Work: More Speed and Stress_ by Mark, Gudith & Klocke (ACM CHI, 2008)](https://ics.uci.edu/~gmark/chi08-mark.pdf)
  被打断者会加速完成但压力更高。Use for: "你以为补回来了，其实是用压力换的"。
- [Book/Site: _Attention Span_ by Gloria Mark (2023)](https://gloriamark.com/)
  官网称屏幕上的平均注意持续约 47 秒且在缩短。Use for: 想系统读的人。
- [Paper: _The challenge of attention residue when switching between work tasks_ by Sophie Leroy (OBHDP, 2009)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obhdp.2009.04.002)
  注意残留概念出处（仅核实题名/期刊/DOI）。Use for: "等 CI 时刷群会毁掉下一个小时"。
- [Paper: _Brain Drain: The Mere Presence of One's Own Smartphone Reduces Available Cognitive Capacity_ by Ward et al. (JACR, 2017)](https://doi.org/10.1086/691462)
  手机仅仅在场就占用认知资源，依赖越深代价越大。Use for: "手机放另一个房间"的依据。
- [Book: _Deep Work_ by Cal Newport (2016)](https://calnewport.com/books/deep-work/)
  通俗综合，适合程序员。Chinese edition: 《深度工作》。Use for: 整块时间的推荐读物。
- [Site: _The Pomodoro Technique_ by Francesco Cirillo（官方）](https://www.pomodorotechnique.com/)
  官方原文：25 分钟专注 + 5 分钟休息（[Classic Timer 页](https://www.pomodorotechnique.com/solutions/pomodoro-classic-timer/)）。Use for: 方法出处。
- [Paper: _Mindfulness training improves working memory capacity and GRE performance while reducing mind wandering_ by Mrazek et al. (Psychological Science, 2013)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23538911/)
  RCT，2 周正念训练减少走神、提高工作记忆。Use for: 正念课的证据。
- [Review: _The neuroscience of mindfulness meditation_ by Tang, Hölzel & Posner (Nature Reviews Neuroscience, 2015)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25783612/)
  有效但机制不清、需要更严格研究。Use for: "有效但别神化"。

### I. 就医提示用

- [Instrument: _Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale (ASRS-v1.1)_ by WHO / Kessler et al.（ADDA 托管 PDF）](https://add.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/adhd-questionnaire-ASRS111.pdf)
